From quote to car-ready

How a job runs

Most driveways take three to five days on site, plus curing time. Here's what happens and when.

1
Day 0

Site visit & quote

We measure up, check levels and access, and send a fixed written quote within 48 hours.

2
Day 1–2

Dig out & formwork

Excavation, compacted road base, formwork set to level, and mesh on bar chairs.

3
Day 3

The pour

The truck arrives early. We place, screed and bull-float while the mix is workable.

4
Day 3–4

Finish & cut

Your chosen finish goes on, then control joints are saw cut to stop random cracking.

5
Day 7–28

Cure & handover

Foot traffic after 24 hrs, cars after 7 days. Full strength is reached at 28 days.
